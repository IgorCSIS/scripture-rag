Load public-domain books in PDF or TXT, ask in English, Portuguese or Spanish, and read the exact passages and pages behind every answer. Explore shared themes across traditions with unsupervised clustering.

Honest

Themes

k-means over passage embeddings, PCA map, TF-IDF topic labels, similar passages across books.

        Unsupervised theme discovery

        Passages are embedded, clustered with k-means (k chosen by silhouette), projected to 2D with PCA and labeled with class-based TF-IDF keywords. The map re-clusters as you add books. The Python app also offers BERTopic (UMAP plus HDBSCAN) and exports an interactive Plotly map (make demo).

        Clusters are statistical similarity between words and passages, not theological or doctrinal claims.

        Measured complexity

        Median of 5 runs on seeded synthetic data for n from 1,000 to 50,000. Dashed lines are the least squares fit of the predicted Big-O.

        Run the full app

        The local Gradio app adds grounded generation with Ollama on your own GPU. A hosted Streamlit demo (extractive mode) can be deployed from the same repository. See the quick start.
